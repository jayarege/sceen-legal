## 8. Connected services

You can connect Sceen to your own account with a third-party app that
supports our connector (for example Claude, made by Anthropic) so that it
can read your Sceen library on your behalf. The App offers one-tap setup
for Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini. Connecting is optional, and
nothing is connected unless you approve it on our consent page. We do
not vet apps that request a connection; approving one is your decision.

- **You must be 18 or older** to connect an app. Accounts in the 13–17
  age bracket are not eligible. §1's parent-or-guardian permission does
  not extend to this feature.
- **What a connected app can do.** It can read your ratings, watchlist,
  "not interested" list, streaming preferences, and age bracket, and
  search title metadata through our server. It can see your friends'
  names, and, when you ask it to pick something to watch with one friend
  who is 18 or older with a public profile, limited information about
  that friend's taste. It can save your score for a movie or show only if
  you turn on "Let AI apps save scores" in the App, up to 100 saves per
  day. Our database rejects every other write from connector
  credentials. A score a connected app saves on your instruction is your
  rating under these terms. Privacy Policy §3 ("Services you connect")
  lists exactly what our connector tools return, what they never return,
  and what the credential itself carries.
- **Their terms, not ours.** The app you connect is run by someone else,
  under its own terms and privacy policy. We are not responsible for
  what that app does with the data you authorize it to read, for what it
  tells you, or for whether it works. Anything it says about your Sceen
  library is its statement, not ours.
- **It acts as you.** Requests a connected app makes with the credential
  you approved count as your use of Sceen under these terms, including
  §4. Keep your account with that app secure, and disconnect Sceen there
  if you stop trusting it.
- **Limits and suspension.** We rate-limit connected-service requests
  per account. We may suspend, limit, or revoke a connection, a
  particular app, or the connected-services feature as a whole — for
  abuse, to protect the App or other users, or because the third-party
  app changes in a way we cannot support — with notice when we can.
- **Disconnecting.** The credential does not expire on its own — it
  renews automatically until you disconnect or we revoke it. Remove the

  name, time, connected app, duration, and row count, never the content

  score, and which app saved it, so you can see and undo it. See Privacy
