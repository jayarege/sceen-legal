Connect Sceen to your AI

Your ratings, watchlist and not-interested list, read by the AI you already pay for. It can only read, never change, your library. The live connector is for people 18 and older. Want the simplest route, or no AI subscription? Export a file instead.

No setup

Export a file

In the Sceen app, go to Profile › Settings › Connect to AI › Export to AI. On Home, you can also tap Use Claude or ChatGPT with your library under AI Recommendations, then Export to AI.

Then upload or paste the file into any AI app. The file tells the AI what Sceen is and what each list means, then asks it to wait for your questions, so ask whatever you like: picks for tonight, stats or charts. It works on any plan, with any AI, and the AI only sees that snapshot.

Questions

What can it read?

Your ratings with scores, your watchlist, your not-interested list, the streaming services you picked, your age bracket, and a summary of your taste (genres, decades). The tools never return your email, your birth year, or other people's data.

Is it read-only?

Yes. The connector gives the AI read tools only: there is no tool to rate, add, change or delete anything. Account actions such as deleting your account also refuse connector credentials.

Who can connect?

People 18 or older with a birth year set in the app (Profile › Settings).

How do I disconnect?

In the AI app's connector settings: Claude under Customize › Connectors, ChatGPT under Plugins, Grok at grok.com/connectors, Gemini under Settings › Connected Apps. The credential renews itself until you disconnect it there.
